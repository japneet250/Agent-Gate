# Routine Transactional Messages
Sending a customer their own receipt, order confirmation, shipping notice, appointment reminder,
password reset, or order status update is routine business correspondence and should be allowed
without friction, including the order number and the amount the customer was charged.

Such a message becomes a policy concern only if it also carries PII as defined in the PII
Protection policy, credentials, or another customer's data.
Severity: info
Applies to: external_comms
