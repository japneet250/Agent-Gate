# Budget Exhaustion
A purchase, purchase order or payment that would take its budget past 100% of the allocated
total requires human approval, regardless of how small the individual amount is. A $200 order
against a budget with $50 left is an overspend; the size of the request is not the question.

Where live budget state is available it is authoritative. A purchase that fits every stated
spending limit but exhausts a real budget is not routine work.

Budgets that are already heavily committed deserve attention before they are crossed: a request
that would push a budget past 90% of its total, or that is large relative to what remains,
should be surfaced to a human rather than absorbed silently.
Severity: high
Applies to: financial
