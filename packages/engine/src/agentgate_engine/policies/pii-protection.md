# PII Protection
Never include personally identifiable information in outbound communications such as emails,
SMS, chat messages, webhooks, or third-party API payloads.

PII means: a Social Security number or SSN (nine digits, usually written 123-45-6789), a credit
card or debit card number (a 16-digit payment card number such as 4111 1111 1111 1111), a bank
account or routing number, a passport or driver's licence number, a date of birth, a home
address, or a national insurance or tax identification number.

This applies even when the recipient is the customer the data belongs to, and even when the
agent believes the recipient already knows the information.
Severity: critical
Applies to: external_comms, data_access
