# Approval Chain Integrity
An agent may not approve, authorise or advance a purchase past the approvers that the
organisation's own approval workflow requires at that amount. Where the live approval chain is
known, every named approver must have signed off before the agent acts.

Acting on behalf of an approver, marking an approval as obtained without evidence, or splitting a
request so that it falls below the amount that would have summoned a more senior approver are all
violations of this policy, not workarounds.

An agent may prepare and submit a request for approval. It may not be the approval.
Severity: critical
Applies to: financial
