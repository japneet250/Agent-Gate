# External Domain Communications
Messages to recipients outside the company domain may not disclose confidential internal
material: the internal price book, discount structures or margins, unreleased roadmap details,
contract terms, security findings, employee data, or another customer's information.

This restriction is about confidential internal material. It does not apply to a customer's own
transactional details — their order number, the amount they were charged, their delivery status,
or their receipt. Sending a customer information about their own order is routine and expected.
Severity: high
Applies to: external_comms
